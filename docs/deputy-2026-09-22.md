## What this repository is

A Lean 4 certification of the uniqueness half of Erdős problem 1007: a graph of
dimension four with nine edges and no isolated vertex is isomorphic to `K₃,₃`.
The statement is `erdos_1007.variants.dimension_four_extremal` from
`google-deepmind/formal-conjectures`, inlined in
`Erdos1007/Standalone/Mathlib/InlineErdos1007.lean`. `Challenge.lean` is generated
from that file. Do not hand-edit `Challenge.lean`.

The mathematics is proved: House, Discrete Math. 313 (2013), and Chaffee–Noble,
Australas. J. Combin. 64(2) (2016), Theorem 7. This project follows Chaffee–Noble.
The blueprint is `blueprint/src/content.tex`.
