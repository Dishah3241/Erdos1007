## Findings

**1 (high, repaired). The `UnitDistanceEmbeddable` separator did not separate.** The docstring
claimed `K₂` plus an isolated vertex in `ℝ¹` witnesses that no placement satisfies the stricter
unit-distance-graph reading. The reviewer kernel-checked the refutation: place the edge at `0, 1`
and the third vertex at `3`, and distance one holds exactly for adjacent pairs. The error was
conflating "some placement has an accidental unit non-edge" with "no placement avoids one" — the
latter is a much stronger claim and not what separating the definitions requires. The proposition
now exhibits a placement with a unit non-edge, which is what shows the definition does not
constrain non-adjacent vertices.

**2 (high, acknowledged, not repaired). The obligations are declared, not discharged.** All four
proofs are `sorry`. Declaring a separating example as a proposition proves nothing, and
`lake exe fidelity` checks only that a companion exists, is about its subject, and is not `True`.
It cannot check that the companion is *true*. This is Stage 3 work and the repository says
plainly that nothing is proved.

The reviewer also noted there is no independent Statement B and no recorded A↔B proof. Correct,
and deliberate: see "Process deviation" below.

**3 (medium, repaired). `HasDimension.separating` admitted the empty graph.** `V = Fin 0`,
`G = ⊥` is representable in every dimension and has dimension zero, so it satisfied the separator
degenerately — kernel-checked. A separating example only the empty object witnesses establishes
nothing. The proposition now requires the graph to have an edge; `K₂` is the intended witness.

**4 (low, repaired 2026-09-21). Stale metadata.** `formalization.yaml` still described the

## Process deviation from `docs/PLAYBOOK.md` stage 1

The playbook calls for two independent statements from different model lineages, proved equivalent
in Lean. That is the right procedure when a statement is **authored**. This statement was
**inherited**: it reproduces a `formal-conjectures` declaration that already carries that
project's review, with two definitions inlined and a notation expanded.

So the equivalence that matters is inlined-versus-upstream, not A-versus-B, and that is what was
checked — by `rfl`, on a different lineage from the one that did the inlining. Authoring a second
statement from the informal claim would have tested something real, and it was not done; the
inherited statement's own provenance is the mitigation. Recorded here rather than left implicit.

It also gave an explicit witness construction for `DimensionFourExtremal.witness`: with
`r = 1/√2`, parts `{(r,0,0,0), (-r,0,0,0), (0,r,0,0)}` and `{(0,0,r,0), (0,0,-r,0), (0,0,0,r)}`

noncollinear centres lies on a line and holds at most two points. This is Lemma 3 of
Chaffee–Noble. It is a route for Stage 3, not a proof.
